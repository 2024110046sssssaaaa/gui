Lecture 6: Concurrency and Synchronization

Race Conditions:
- Multiple processes/threads access shared data
- Outcome depends on timing of execution
- Can lead to inconsistent or incorrect results

Synchronization Mechanisms:
- Locks (Mutexes): Mutual exclusion for critical sections
- Semaphores: Counter-based signaling mechanism
- Monitors: High-level abstraction with condition variables

Deadlock:
- Four conditions: mutual exclusion, hold and wait, no preemption, circular wait
- Prevention strategies: break one of the conditions
- Detection and recovery algorithms
